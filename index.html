01 — About

Building instruments that
measure light itself

I'm an Optical Engineer with a Master's in Advanced Physics (Photonics) from the University of Valencia. My work lives at the intersection of experimental optics, computational imaging, and precision metrology.

My MSc thesis — awarded with maximum grade — documents the full design and implementation of a telecentric NIR Digital Holographic Microscope capable of characterising nano-structured metasurfaces with sub-wavelength phase sensitivity.

I enjoy building things from scratch: aligning optical benches, writing reconstruction algorithms, and turning raw interference fringes into quantitative phase maps.

02 — Research

Focus Areas

◈

Digital Holographic Microscopy

Design and implementation of telecentric off-axis DHM systems for quantitative phase imaging (QPI). My thesis system achieved a lateral phase resolution of 0.07 rad (~λ/90), enabling nano-structure discrimination.

DHMQPIInterferometry
◈

Metasurface Characterisation

Experimental validation of DHM metrological performance applied to multiple dielectric metasurfaces within the EU-funded DISCAMNIL project (NextGeneration EU). Precise phase discrimination of sub-wavelength nano-structures.

MetasurfacesNanophotonicsMetrology
◈

NIR Optical Systems

Design and alignment of near-infrared optical instruments. Experience with telecentric and afocal configurations, beam-splitter Mach–Zehnder geometries, and camera-coupled interferometric setups.

NIRZemaxOptical Design
◈

Computational Imaging

Development of hologram reconstruction and phase-unwrapping software pipelines. Numerical propagation methods (angular spectrum, Fresnel), spatial filtering, and automated phase-aberration compensation.
